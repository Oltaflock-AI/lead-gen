Quality score formula
score = 100 × (
  0.40 × max(0, (rating − 3) / 2)            # 3.0★ → 0, 5.0★ → 1
  + 0.20 × min(1, log10(reviews + 1) / 3)    # ~1000 reviews → 1
  + 0.15 × has_email
  + 0.15 × has_phone
  + 0.10 × no_website
)
Qualified threshold: score ≥ 60. Manual pipeline = qualified × close_rate × avg_deal_value. AI forecast (if enabled) replaces this with Claude's per-niche estimate.
