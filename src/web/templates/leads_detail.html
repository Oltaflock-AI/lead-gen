How AI scoring works (click to expand)
Two independent AI scores (0-100 each), generated by Claude in batches of 50:
  • Niche fit — how well the BUSINESS NICHE matches AI voice agent / chatbot / missed-call recovery. 80+ for appointment-driven service businesses (plumbers, dentists, lawyers, salons), 60-79 for hybrid bookers (real estate, restaurants), <40 for pure retail.
  • AI lead score — will THIS specific business respond to cold email and become a customer? Anchored to a typical solid local biz (4.6★, 60 reviews, email + phone) at 65-75. Boost: many reviews + high rating + both contact channels. Penalize: missing contact info, low rating with high review count, very small business.
Click any chip to see the AI's reason in the tooltip. The score is rerun whenever you click Score all with AI — it overwrites the previous score. Outreach page now uses the same AI lead score (no more separate rule-based score).
Three Enrich variants — what's the difference?
Enrich (default) — for every row: hit Google Places, add phone + verified address + rating + reviews + maps URL, scrape & MX-verify an email. Drops rows that have a website, no phone, or are permanently closed. Use this when you want a clean cold-call list.
Enrich (keep w/ websites) — same as default but does not drop rows that have a website. Use this when you're pitching AI services to existing online businesses.
Enrich (skip email) — same as default but skips the slow DDG search + MX-verify step. Faster; emails stay blank. Pair with the standalone Scrape + verify emails button above to do email lookup separately.
